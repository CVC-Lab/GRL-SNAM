GRL-SNAM

Geometric reinforcement learning for navigation and mapping in unknown environments: navigation as energy minimization with symplectic (Hamiltonian) dynamics, a learned policy that sets the energy's coefficients, and radius-aware collision avoidance with IPC barriers.

Source on GitHub · Developer guide · The Ariadne ports

In your browser

These pages run GRL-SNAM's own Python in the page: CPython 3.12 and numpy compiled to WebAssembly, with libcvc's Ariadne documents for the windows (libcvc's ariglx-web). Nothing is installed and nothing leaves the page. The first visit downloads about 35 MB, which the browser then caches. A desktop browser with WebGL 2 works best.

Did it learn?

grl-snam ariadne learn on the five training runs of the learning check: each held-out curve against its training twin with its marks (best, elbow, plateau, divergence), the policy's heads with their spread, gradient norms, the checkpoint table, and grl-snam learn-report's claims, each pass or fail with its evidence. The page reads the runs' learning histories and makes the claims itself.

Watch it learn

grl-snam ariadne scrub on the learning check's runs: every checkpoint each run saved, deployed on one scenario in libcvc's generated city. The Set combo picks the first retrain, the retrain after the fix, or the one in world units (five seeds each), at the same seed and step. Pick checkpoint k with the slider, the combo, Previous and Next, or Most apart; its Humvee drives beside step 0's over the episode, with the Stats and Ledger windows for the two, a line saying how far apart the two drive, and a table of what each checkpoint did: reached, time, path, ticks penetrating, clearance, what was left to the goal, how far it got from step 0. Where every checkpoint drives within a car's width of step 0, the page says so: then scrubbing shows nothing, because training changed nothing you can see. It opens on the set whose checkpoints part most, at the one farthest from step 0. The Plant combo switches between the Jolt Humvee, which stops at the buildings, and nav's kinematic bicycle, which drives through them. The runs are deployed when the site is built (that needs torch); the page plays them back, and adds about 95 MB to the first visit. How to read it.

CoPH: agents that learn and share

CoPH-Stagewise in 3-D

CoPH-Sense's method, carried in GRL-SNAM as grl_snam.coph: navigation energies learned stage by stage, kept only through a validation gate, and shared between agents as small packets. The page has the walkthrough of its crossing and hazard demos, with communication off beside CoPH on the same seed. It gives the two results the reference establishes, with their seed counts, and what it does not establish. The live demo in the page will follow.

Navigation on libcvc

GRL-SNAM's reactive navigation also runs natively on libcvc's cvc::nav runtime, with no Python and no libtorch. These demos are in libcvc's gallery.

  • nav_city_swarm: hundreds of vehicles cross a foggy city with no global plan; tune the agents, the fog and shared or private belief live.
  • nav_city_drive: convoys drive downtown Austin to rally points on the published policy; follow a lead vehicle, or drag a rally point to re-route a convoy.
  • nav_fog_ghost: a vehicle's stale map shows a wall that is not there; it detours, then erases the wall as its sensor clears the ground.
